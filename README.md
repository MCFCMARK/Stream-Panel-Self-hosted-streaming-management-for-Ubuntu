Stream Panel — Self-hosted streaming management for Ubuntu
Stream Panel is a self-hosted web panel for managing live streams, movies, and TV series from your own Ubuntu server. You can organise content into categories and bouquets, manage users and devices, and assign playback to additional servers as your setup grows.
What you can do
- Manage live streams and VOD from one dashboard.
- Import M3U, JSON, or CSV playlists and assign content to categories and bouquets.
- Automatically refresh playlists with Playlist Sync.
- Add playback servers and choose which server handles each stream.
- Manage users and active connections, including connections on playback servers.
- Extend the panel with optional plugins and proxies, and build a branded Android app from the panel.
Get started
Stream Panel is free to install. Paid update access covers 12 months, with renewal available for further updates. Some optional add-ons are sold separately.

Open the installer page and follow the instructions from your Ubuntu server’s SSH session.
